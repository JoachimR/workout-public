# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-17**

## Who is responsible

Joachim Reiß (reiss@bryter.io) is the controller for the Workout Tracker app within the meaning of the GDPR.

## What data the app collects

Workout Tracker does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet connection and makes no network requests.

All data you enter (exercises, sets, reps, weights, notes) is stored **only on your device** using a local database.

## Android system backup

If you have Android's automatic backup enabled on your device, your workout data may be backed up to your Google account as part of the standard Android backup mechanism. This backup is managed entirely by Google and your device settings — Workout Tracker does not initiate, control, or have access to this backup. Please refer to [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

## Export files

When you use the Backup / Restore feature, export files are written to your device's local storage only. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

## Third-party SDKs

The app uses Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases (optional tip/donation). Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. No payment data is accessible to or stored by the app.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Since all data stays on your device and is not accessible to the developer, there is nothing to access, rectify, or delete on our end. You can delete all app data at any time through your device's app settings, or by uninstalling the app.

For any questions, contact: reiss@bryter.io
